# Monetization

This document outlines ways the relocation dashboard could become a business without compromising trust, methodology, or user safety.

The product's monetization should come from better research, personalization, exports, and updates, not from manipulating rankings.

## Monetization Thesis

People are likely to pay when they face a real relocation, residency, investment, family, or risk-management decision.

The free product can help users explore. The paid product should help users make a more serious shortlist and prepare for conversations with qualified legal, tax, immigration, banking, healthcare, or investment professionals.

## Trust Rules

- Do not sell country ranking placement.
- Do not hide material risks in paid-only sections if the free score would become misleading.
- Do not accept undisclosed affiliate relationships.
- Do not let service-provider partnerships influence scores.
- Do not describe output as legal, tax, immigration, investment, safety, or climate advice.
- Always preserve confidence, source notes, data gaps, and freshness.

## Potential Products

### 1. Paid Country Briefs

One-off researched reports for a specific country.

Includes:

- Full category scoring.
- Profile score breakdowns.
- Source links.
- Data gaps.
- Strengths and weaknesses.
- Residency/tax/banking caveats.
- Suggested professional questions to ask.

Target users:

- Plan B Americans.
- Remote workers.
- Families.
- Retirees.
- Wealth-preservation planners.

Possible pricing:

- $19-$49 for lightweight researched brief.
- $99-$199 for deeper country dossier.

Pros:

- Simple to sell.
- Aligns with research work.
- Good first monetization test.

Risks:

- Requires source freshness discipline.
- Users may expect advice if language is not careful.

### 2. Custom Shortlist Report

A personalized report comparing countries against a user's profile.

Inputs:

- Citizenship.
- Budget.
- Time horizon.
- Family status.
- Remote-work needs.
- Risk tolerance.
- Climate concerns.
- Healthcare needs.
- Tax/banking complexity.
- Preferred regions/languages.

Output:

- 3-7 country shortlist.
- Why each country fits.
- Why each country may fail.
- Profile-specific ranking.
- Research gaps.
- Professional questions to ask.

Possible pricing:

- $249-$750 for a written custom shortlist.
- Higher for advisor-assisted or professional versions.

Pros:

- Strong user value.
- Highest early revenue potential.
- Does not require building user accounts first.

Risks:

- Scope creep.
- Must avoid personalized legal/tax/immigration advice.
- Requires careful disclaimers and intake boundaries.

### 3. Research Library Subscription

Paid access to researched country dossiers and updates.

Includes:

- Full country pages.
- Source links.
- Update history.
- New country releases.
- Profile-specific rankings.
- Comparison tools.
- Data freshness indicators.

Possible pricing:

- $9-$19/month for individual users.
- $99-$199/year annual plan.

Pros:

- Recurring revenue.
- Aligns with ongoing source updates.
- Good if country library grows.

Risks:

- Needs enough researched countries to justify subscription.
- Requires a steady update cadence.
- Free/paid boundary must not make free scores misleading.

### 4. Professional / Advisor Tier

For advisors, family offices, relocation consultants, tax/immigration professionals, and researchers.

Includes:

- Structured country data.
- Exportable reports.
- Methodology documentation.
- Source freshness dashboard.
- Client-ready comparison PDFs.
- Team access later.

Possible pricing:

- $49-$199/month.
- Custom pricing for firms.

Pros:

- Higher willingness to pay.
- Values source transparency.
- Less dependent on consumer SEO.

Risks:

- Requires high data quality.
- May need stronger legal disclaimers and terms.
- Professionals will notice weak methodology quickly.

### 5. Exportable Reports

Paid exports from comparison or country pages.

Formats:

- PDF.
- Markdown.
- CSV/JSON for professional users.

Use cases:

- Take to lawyer.
- Take to CPA.
- Discuss with spouse/family.
- Save research snapshot.

Possible pricing:

- $9-$29 per export.
- Included in subscription.

Pros:

- Directly tied to user workflow.
- Lower commitment than subscription.

Risks:

- Exports must preserve caveats and source notes.

### 6. Alerts and Update Monitoring

Paid notifications when key country conditions change.

Alert categories:

- Residency rules.
- Tax changes.
- Banking/capital controls.
- Safety advisories.
- Climate/insurance signals.
- Elections/policy shifts.

Possible pricing:

- Included in subscription.
- Premium alert tier for professionals.

Pros:

- Strong recurring value.
- Matches source freshness model.

Risks:

- Requires reliable monitoring workflow.
- Live monitoring should not be built until static research quality is strong.

### 7. Affiliate or Referral Revenue

Possible but risky.

Potential partners:

- Books.
- Data products.
- Travel insurance.
- Background check/apostille services.
- Vetted consultation marketplaces.

Avoid early:

- Real estate agents.
- Visa mills.
- Tax schemes.
- Immigration firms that pay for country promotion.
- Any partner tied to ranking placement.

Rules:

- Disclose all referrals.
- Never let referrals affect scores.
- Never recommend a service as required unless verified.
- Prefer user-paid research over provider-paid promotion.

## Free vs Paid Boundary

### Free Should Include

- Basic rankings.
- Country summaries.
- Methodology.
- Confidence levels.
- Major strengths and risks.
- Data freshness status.
- Source-gap visibility.
- Demo/review status.

### Paid Can Include

- Full researched dossiers.
- Full source lists and update history.
- Custom weighting profiles.
- Saved shortlists.
- Exportable reports.
- Custom shortlist reports.
- Alerts.
- Professional data exports.

Important rule:

Free content can be limited, but it must not be misleading. If a country has major caveats, those caveats cannot be hidden behind a paywall while the free ranking remains visible.

## Pricing Hypotheses

### Hypothesis A: One-Off Reports First

Start with paid country briefs and custom shortlists before subscriptions.

Why:

- Easier to validate demand.
- No need for account system.
- Higher value per purchase.
- Helps discover what users actually ask for.

### Hypothesis B: Subscription After Library Depth

Launch subscription only after enough researched countries exist.

Minimum likely threshold:

- 15-25 researched countries.
- Clear update cadence.
- Public methodology.
- Data freshness dashboard.

### Hypothesis C: Professional Tier Later

Professional tier becomes viable once:

- Data model is stable.
- Exports work.
- Source freshness is reliable.
- Methodology is defensible.
- Country library is broad enough.

## Recommended Monetization Sequence

### Phase 1: Free Research Tool

Goal:

- Build trust.
- Validate that users engage with profile switching, country pages, source notes, and comparisons.

Do:

- Public methodology.
- 5-10 researched countries.
- Research queue and review dashboard.
- Email/contact interest form later.

Do not:

- Add payments yet.
- Sell subscriptions with mostly demo data.

### Phase 2: Paid Custom Shortlist

Goal:

- Test willingness to pay.
- Learn user decision criteria.

Offer:

- Manual custom shortlist report.
- Limited slots.
- Clear disclaimer.

### Phase 3: Paid Country Briefs

Goal:

- Productize research output.

Offer:

- Downloadable country reports.
- Full source notes.
- Professional question checklist.

### Phase 4: Subscription Library

Goal:

- Recurring revenue from maintained research.

Offer:

- Full country library.
- Updates.
- Saved comparisons.
- Custom profiles.

### Phase 5: Professional Tier

Goal:

- Serve advisors and firms.

Offer:

- Exports.
- Structured data.
- Client-ready reports.
- Source freshness dashboard.

## Monetization Metrics

Track:

- Profile switches per user.
- Country pages viewed.
- Profile cards expanded.
- Source sections expanded.
- Comparisons created.
- Research queue usage.
- Email/report interest clicks.
- Custom report requests.
- Paid conversion by persona.
- Refund or dissatisfaction reasons.

## Ethical Risks

- Users may make life-changing decisions from incomplete information.
- Paid reports may be mistaken for advice.
- Affiliate partners may bias recommendations.
- Countries may be unfairly penalized due to weak data availability.
- Fast-changing rules may become stale quickly.

Mitigations:

- Strong disclaimers.
- Visible source freshness.
- Human review status.
- Professional-question checklists.
- No hidden ranking manipulation.
- Conservative confidence scoring.

## Recommended First Paid Offer

The best first monetization experiment is:

Custom Plan B Shortlist Report

Positioning:

For Americans who are not sure they want to leave, but want a credible backup-country shortlist based on their risk profile, budget, family needs, work situation, and time horizon.

Deliverable:

- 5-7 country shortlist.
- Profile-specific ranking.
- Main fit reasons.
- Main failure modes.
- Source gaps.
- Questions to ask immigration/tax/legal professionals.

Why this first:

- High value.
- Low engineering overhead.
- Learns what users need.
- Does not require a large researched-country library before testing demand.
