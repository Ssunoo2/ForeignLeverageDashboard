# User Stories

These user stories focus on why someone might want or need to leave their current country, how they discover unfamiliar destination countries, and what the dashboard should help them decide.

The product should not assume everyone is chasing the same “best country.” Different users are trying to reduce different risks, preserve different freedoms, and build different kinds of optionality.

## Core Product Promise

Help users move from vague relocation anxiety to a structured, transparent shortlist of countries that fit their personal risk profile, life stage, time horizon, and practical constraints.

The site should make it normal to say:

- “I do not know if I want to move yet.”
- “I need a backup plan.”
- “I want to compare countries I have never seriously considered.”
- “I want the reasons, not just a ranking.”
- “I need to see uncertainty before trusting a score.”

## Personas

### 1. The Plan B American

This user is not necessarily ready to leave. They feel political, social, economic, or institutional instability rising and want credible backup options before they are forced to act.

Stories:

- As a politically anxious American, I want to compare countries by legal residency, safety, civil liberties, and exit optionality so I can build a Plan B before a crisis.
- As someone worried about polarization and institutional decline, I want countries scored by political stability and civil liberties so I can identify places that feel calmer without being authoritarian.
- As someone who has never lived abroad, I want clear explanations of unfamiliar countries so I can consider options beyond the usual Portugal/Mexico/Panama lists.
- As someone not ready to move yet, I want to save or revisit a shortlist so I can gradually research instead of making an emotional decision.

Feature implications:

- Balanced Relocation profile
- Civil liberties and political stability filters
- Beginner-friendly country summaries
- “Why this country might surprise you” sections
- Watchlist or shortlist later

Monetization signal:

- Paid “Plan B shortlist” report
- Subscription for country updates and new destination briefs

### 2. The Remote Worker / Entrepreneur

This user earns online or can move income across borders. They may be leaving high costs, taxes, burnout, crime, or regulatory frustration.

Stories:

- As a remote worker priced out of my city, I want to compare cost of living, internet reliability, banking, and time-zone fit so I can find a place where my income goes further.
- As a founder or consultant, I want to know whether I can legally live, work, bank, invoice, and use payment processors in a country before I consider relocating.
- As someone who only knows popular nomad hubs, I want to discover less obvious countries that might be better long-term bases.
- As a business owner, I want to see tax caveats and source confidence so I know when I need professional advice.

Feature implications:

- Remote Worker / Entrepreneur profile
- Payment processor and banking notes
- Internet and infrastructure metrics
- Cost-of-living freshness indicators
- Tax caveat warnings

Monetization signal:

- Paid remote-worker country comparison
- Custom entrepreneur relocation report
- Exportable country brief for professional review

### 3. The Wealth Preservation Planner

This user is concerned about capital controls, taxation, inflation, banking instability, property rights, litigation, or unpredictable policy.

Stories:

- As someone worried about capital controls or banking fragility, I want countries ranked by banking stability, property rights, legal system, and exit optionality.
- As a U.S. citizen with foreign-account complexity, I want every banking and tax score to include caveats so I do not mistake a favorable local regime for a complete answer.
- As someone considering foreign property, I want to see whether ownership rights, title systems, insurance, and liquidity have been reviewed.
- As someone who has heard relocation influencers promote a country, I want to see source quality and data gaps before trusting the opportunity.

Feature implications:

- Wealth Preservation profile
- Source-tier visibility
- Property-rights detail pages
- Banking/FATCA caveats
- Data quality score separate from country attractiveness

Monetization signal:

- Paid wealth-preservation dossiers
- Professional tier for advisors or family offices
- Country-by-country due diligence exports

### 4. The Family Stability Seeker

This user may be leaving crime, school concerns, healthcare anxiety, social instability, or a poor environment for children.

Stories:

- As a parent, I want to compare safety, healthcare, schools, infrastructure, and daily friction so I can find countries that might work for my family.
- As someone with children, I want to know which countries require private schools, international schools, or local-language fluency.
- As a parent worried about crime or social disorder, I want city and regional caveats, not just national safety scores.
- As someone planning years ahead, I want to know whether a country works for a test stay, a one-year base, and a five-year family plan.

Feature implications:

- Family and Aging profile
- Family suitability category
- City/regional caveats
- School and healthcare source gaps
- Time horizon fit

Monetization signal:

- Paid family relocation shortlist
- School/healthcare focused country add-ons
- Premium comparison reports for households

### 5. The Climate and Resilience Planner

This user is worried about heat, drought, fire, insurance collapse, food security, energy security, geopolitical stress, or supply chains.

Stories:

- As someone worried about climate risk, I want to compare countries by climate resilience, food, water, energy, state capacity, and tail risk.
- As someone considering a “climate haven,” I want to see water, insurance, grid, and regional caveats before accepting the label.
- As someone planning for 10-30 years, I want to distinguish countries that are pleasant today from countries that may remain livable under stress.
- As someone who has not considered countries like Uruguay, Chile, Paraguay, or Argentina as climate or resource hedges, I want the site to explain why they might matter.

Feature implications:

- Climate Hedge profile
- Regional climate caveats
- Insurance signals
- Food/water/energy security
- Long-horizon scoring

Monetization signal:

- Paid climate resilience reports
- Source freshness subscription for climate/resource categories
- Custom resilience shortlist

### 6. The “Soft Exit” Explorer

This user does not want to permanently emigrate. They want a second base, test stay, backup residency, or optionality.

Stories:

- As someone curious but uncertain, I want to find countries where I can test living for 1-3 months before committing.
- As someone with family and work still in the U.S., I want countries that are reachable, legally workable, and not too culturally difficult.
- As someone who wants optionality rather than escape, I want countries scored by residency, exit optionality, global connectivity, and daily friction.
- As someone who has never considered South America seriously, I want the dashboard to show tradeoffs in plain language.

Feature implications:

- Time horizon fit
- Test-stay suitability
- Global connectivity
- Daily friction category
- “Good for test stay vs long-term base” labels

Monetization signal:

- Paid test-stay planning guide
- Custom shortlist by travel constraints
- Premium country comparison exports

### 7. The Values and Freedom Migrant

This user may be leaving because of speech concerns, personal autonomy, medical-policy concerns, surveillance, censorship, cultural alienation, or social pressure.

Stories:

- As someone worried about freedom and autonomy, I want to compare civil liberties, digital freedom, legal system, and state capacity.
- As someone leaving a place that feels culturally hostile, I want to understand whether a destination is psychologically and socially sustainable.
- As someone concerned about authoritarianism, I want to distinguish competent states from controlling states.
- As someone worried about being an outsider, I want to see foreigner backlash, language fit, and community-network notes.

Feature implications:

- Civil liberties and digital freedom detail
- Psychological fit category
- Foreigner backlash category
- Cultural/language caveats
- Community network notes

Monetization signal:

- Paid freedom/values relocation brief
- Subscription country updates for civil liberties and digital freedom

### 8. The Retiree or Aging Planner

This user may be leaving high healthcare costs, unsafe cities, loneliness, unaffordable housing, or fear of aging without support.

Stories:

- As a future retiree, I want to compare healthcare, safety, cost of living, aging suitability, and family visitation logistics.
- As someone with chronic health concerns, I want to know whether care is available outside the capital city.
- As someone priced out of comfortable retirement, I want to identify countries where private healthcare and domestic help may be affordable.
- As someone who may become less mobile, I want to know if the country still works at age 75, not just age 35.

Feature implications:

- Family and Aging profile
- Aging suitability category
- Healthcare detail with city caveats
- Home care / assisted living source gaps
- Global connectivity for family visits

Monetization signal:

- Paid retirement shortlist report
- Healthcare and aging add-on reports

### 9. The High-Upside Frontier Seeker

This user is not looking for maximum comfort. They are willing to accept volatility in exchange for opportunity, underpriced assets, reform momentum, or resource exposure.

Stories:

- As a frontier-minded investor or builder, I want to identify countries with upside, low costs, resource security, and tolerable risk.
- As someone considering volatile countries, I want the dashboard to show currency, banking, political, and tail risks clearly.
- As someone intrigued by countries others dismiss, I want to see whether the risk/reward is actually favorable or just hype.
- As a founder, investor, or operator, I want to compare opportunity against daily friction and legal certainty.

Feature implications:

- Frontier Upside profile
- Risk tolerance fit
- Tail risk
- Economy opportunity
- Data quality / reality gap category
- Hype warnings

Monetization signal:

- Paid frontier country briefs
- Investor-oriented country dossiers
- Early access to new country research

### 10. The Research Editor

This user maintains the dataset. They care about evidence quality, freshness, consistency, and whether the app overstates confidence.

Stories:

- As a research editor, I want to see which categories are missing, stale, Low confidence, or source-poor so I know what to research next.
- As a reviewer, I want category scores to show source notes and data gaps before a country is marked reviewed.
- As an editor, I want researched countries separated from demo countries so public readers are not misled.
- As a maintainer, I want the app to compute profile scores from category data so I do not manually update rankings.

Feature implications:

- Research queue
- Review dashboard
- Data quality score
- Key metrics/source cards
- Validation tests

Monetization signal:

- Internal editorial tooling supports paid brief quality
- Professional-grade trust layer for subscribers

## Discovery Stories

These stories focus on users finding countries they had not considered.

- As a user who only knows popular expat destinations, I want the dashboard to surface countries that match my profile even if they are not trendy.
- As someone skeptical of unfamiliar countries, I want to see why a country ranks well and what its weaknesses are.
- As someone who has heard scary headlines about a region, I want category-level evidence so I can distinguish real risk from vague reputation.
- As someone who has heard glowing influencer claims, I want source quality and data gaps so I can distinguish real opportunity from marketing.
- As someone comparing two unfamiliar countries, I want a side-by-side view that explains tradeoffs in plain English.

Feature implications:

- “Why this country appears” explanation
- “Surprising fits” section
- Country comparison summaries
- Evidence-first ranking tables
- Hype/risk warning badges

## Monetization-Oriented Stories

These stories indicate what a user might pay for.

- As a serious relocation planner, I want a personalized shortlist based on my profile, constraints, and risk tolerance.
- As a paid subscriber, I want access to full country dossiers, source links, update history, and category rationales.
- As a family office or advisor, I want structured country data and exportable reports for client conversations.
- As a remote worker, I want a country comparison report that I can take to my tax, legal, or immigration advisors.
- As a user tracking unstable conditions, I want alerts when residency, tax, banking, safety, or climate data changes materially.

Possible paid products:

- Paid country briefs
- Subscription research library
- Custom relocation shortlist report
- Professional/advisor tier
- Exportable PDF or Markdown country dossiers
- Source freshness and update alerts

## MVP User Stories

The current static dashboard should prioritize these first:

- As a user, I can select a weighting profile and see how country rankings change.
- As a user, I can open a country and see category scores, rationales, confidence, source notes, and data gaps.
- As a user, I can expand profile scores and understand which categories contributed to the result.
- As a user, I can compare countries side by side.
- As a research editor, I can see which country files are demo, incomplete, Low confidence, or source-poor.
- As a research editor, I can see the next research tasks before marking a country reviewed.

## Product Guardrails

- Do not market scores as objective truth.
- Do not imply legal, tax, immigration, investment, safety, or climate certainty.
- Do not let monetization bias rankings.
- Do not hide source gaps from free users in a way that makes free content misleading.
- Do not promote a country as “best” without explaining who it is best for and who it is bad for.
- Do not allow affiliate relationships to determine country recommendations.

## Strategic Product Direction

The strongest version of this product is not a travel site. It is a relocation intelligence and jurisdiction-planning tool.

Its differentiator should be:

- category-level scoring
- transparent uncertainty
- multiple user profiles
- source quality
- data freshness
- serious but readable country discovery

The site should help users discover countries they had not considered while also making it clear when a country needs more research before it belongs on a real shortlist.
