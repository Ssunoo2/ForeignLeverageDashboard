# Personas

These personas describe users who may want or need to leave where they are from, build a backup plan, or discover countries they have not seriously considered.

The product should support people who are curious, anxious, strategic, or already actively planning. It should not assume that every user wants to permanently emigrate.

## Persona Summary

| Persona | Primary Need | Time Horizon | Likely Paid Interest |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Plan B American | Backup residency and optionality | 6 months-10 years | Custom shortlist, update subscription |
| Remote Worker / Entrepreneur | Lower costs, legal stay, banking, online work | 1 month-5 years | Country comparison, entrepreneur report |
| Wealth Preservation Planner | Capital safety, property rights, banking, tax caveats | 1-20 years | Dossiers, professional tier |
| Family Stability Seeker | Safety, schools, healthcare, household durability | 1-10 years | Family relocation report |
| Climate and Resilience Planner | Long-term livability, resources, adaptation | 5-30 years | Climate/resilience report |
| Soft Exit Explorer | Test stay, second base, optionality | 1 month-3 years | Test-stay guide, shortlist |
| Values and Freedom Migrant | Civil liberties, autonomy, cultural fit | 6 months-10 years | Freedom/values brief |
| Retiree or Aging Planner | Healthcare, safety, cost, aging support | 1-20 years | Retirement shortlist, healthcare add-on |
| Frontier Upside Seeker | Underpriced opportunity with tolerable risk | 6 months-10 years | Frontier country briefs |
| Research Editor | Data quality, source review, consistency | Ongoing | Internal/professional tooling |

## 1. Plan B American

### Context

This user is worried about political instability, social polarization, institutional decline, economic shocks, crime, or cultural fracture. They may not want to leave, but they want a credible backup plan before circumstances force a rushed decision.

### Motivations

- Build legal residency optionality before a crisis.
- Identify stable countries outside familiar expat marketing loops.
- Compare political stability, safety, civil liberties, and exit options.
- Reduce anxiety by converting vague concern into a concrete shortlist.

### Fears

- Waiting too long and losing good options.
- Choosing a country based on influencer hype.
- Discovering too late that residency, banking, or taxes are not practical.
- Moving from one unstable system into another.

### Decision Triggers

- Political unrest or extreme polarization.
- New laws affecting speech, taxes, guns, healthcare, privacy, business, or schooling.
- Local crime or quality-of-life deterioration.
- A friend or peer successfully obtaining foreign residency.

### Countries They May Already Know

Portugal, Mexico, Costa Rica, Panama, Canada, Spain.

### Countries They May Overlook

Uruguay, Chile, Paraguay, Argentina, Georgia, Malaysia, Slovenia.

### Product Needs

- Balanced Relocation ranking.
- Clear “good for / bad for” summaries.
- Residency and optionality visibility.
- Confidence and source caveats.
- Beginner-friendly explanations for unfamiliar countries.

## 2. Remote Worker / Entrepreneur

### Context

This user can earn online or operate a portable business. They may be leaving high costs, burnout, regulation, taxes, crime, or poor quality of life.

### Motivations

- Increase quality of life per dollar.
- Stay in compatible time zones.
- Maintain reliable internet, banking, payments, and business operations.
- Find places that work for both test stays and longer bases.

### Fears

- Discovering they cannot legally work or stay long enough.
- Losing access to bank accounts, payment processors, or business tools.
- Underestimating tax complexity.
- Choosing a cheap country with too much daily friction.

### Decision Triggers

- Rent increase or burnout in a major metro.
- Remote-work permanence.
- Business becoming location independent.
- Desire to lower burn rate.

### Countries They May Already Know

Mexico, Portugal, Spain, Thailand, Colombia, Costa Rica.

### Countries They May Overlook

Uruguay, Chile, Paraguay, Argentina, Albania, Georgia, Malaysia.

### Product Needs

- Remote Worker / Entrepreneur profile.
- Time-zone and internet indicators.
- Banking/payment caveats.
- Tax warnings.
- Cost-of-living freshness.

## 3. Wealth Preservation Planner

### Context

This user is concerned about inflation, capital controls, banking fragility, litigation, taxation, property rights, or unpredictable policy. They may be high-income, high-net-worth, or simply cautious.

### Motivations

- Diversify jurisdictional risk.
- Understand property ownership and legal-system reliability.
- Avoid trapped capital.
- Separate local tax attractiveness from U.S. citizen obligations.

### Fears

- Capital controls or banking freezes.
- Weak courts or unreliable title.
- Fake “tax haven” claims.
- Affiliate-driven relocation advice.

### Decision Triggers

- Bank instability.
- New taxes or reporting rules.
- Property market concerns.
- Desire to hold foreign residency or property as a hedge.

### Countries They May Already Know

Switzerland, Singapore, UAE, Panama, Portugal.

### Countries They May Overlook

Uruguay, Chile, Andorra, Malta, Georgia, Paraguay.

### Product Needs

- Wealth Preservation profile.
- Banking, property rights, legal system, and tax treatment detail.
- Source-tier visibility.
- Strong disclaimers and professional-review prompts.
- Data quality separated from attractiveness.

## 4. Family Stability Seeker

### Context

This user is concerned about safety, schools, healthcare, social disorder, childcare, or raising children in a deteriorating environment.

### Motivations

- Find a safer and calmer household base.
- Understand school options and family healthcare.
- Evaluate cities and regions, not only national averages.
- Plan for a multi-year family move.

### Fears

- Moving children into worse schools or unsafe areas.
- Underestimating language barriers.
- Losing access to good healthcare.
- Choosing a place that works for adults but not children.

### Decision Triggers

- School dissatisfaction.
- Crime near home.
- Healthcare or childcare costs.
- Desire for a different social environment.

### Countries They May Already Know

Portugal, Spain, Costa Rica, Canada, New Zealand.

### Countries They May Overlook

Uruguay, Chile, Slovenia, Czechia, Malaysia.

### Product Needs

- Family and Aging profile.
- School, healthcare, safety, infrastructure, and daily friction detail.
- Regional caveats.
- Time horizon fit.
- Private/public school and healthcare source gaps.

## 5. Climate and Resilience Planner

### Context

This user is thinking 10-30 years ahead. They worry about heat, drought, fire, flood, food security, water stress, energy systems, insurance collapse, and geopolitical instability.

### Motivations

- Find places likely to remain livable under stress.
- Avoid simplistic “climate haven” narratives.
- Compare food, water, energy, state capacity, and insurance signals.
- Discover resource-secure countries that are not popular relocation brands.

### Fears

- Buying into a climate haven myth.
- Moving somewhere with hidden water, fire, flood, or insurance risks.
- Ignoring state capacity and infrastructure.
- Underestimating regional variation.

### Decision Triggers

- Local climate disasters.
- Insurance premium spikes or insurer withdrawal.
- Heat, drought, fire, or water restrictions.
- Long-term family planning.

### Countries They May Already Know

New Zealand, Canada, Norway, Portugal.

### Countries They May Overlook

Uruguay, Chile, Argentina, Slovenia, parts of Spain, parts of Eastern Europe.

### Product Needs

- Climate Hedge profile.
- Climate plus resource-security categories.
- Regional caveats.
- Insurance signals.
- Long-horizon notes.

## 6. Soft Exit Explorer

### Context

This user wants optionality, not necessarily permanent emigration. They may want a test stay, second base, residency path, or backup option.

### Motivations

- Try living abroad without burning bridges.
- Keep U.S. work/family ties.
- Understand which countries are good for test stays versus permanent bases.
- Build optionality slowly.

### Fears

- Overcommitting too early.
- Choosing a country that is fine for vacation but bad for living.
- Getting trapped by property, bureaucracy, or banking.
- Cultural isolation.

### Decision Triggers

- Flexible work.
- Desire for a winter base.
- Political anxiety without immediate move plans.
- Friends moving abroad.

### Countries They May Already Know

Mexico, Portugal, Costa Rica, Spain.

### Countries They May Overlook

Uruguay, Argentina, Chile, Panama, Georgia, Albania.

### Product Needs

- Test-stay suitability.
- Time horizon fit.
- Daily friction and connectivity.
- Residency pathway clarity.
- Plain-language tradeoff summaries.

## 7. Values and Freedom Migrant

### Context

This user is motivated by civil liberties, speech, medical autonomy, privacy, digital freedom, cultural fit, or moral discomfort with their home environment.

### Motivations

- Find a country that feels freer or less socially coercive.
- Compare civil liberties and digital freedom.
- Avoid authoritarian competence.
- Understand outsider stress, language fit, and local backlash.

### Fears

- Trading one kind of control for another.
- Moving somewhere culturally isolating.
- Overestimating rights on paper.
- Becoming an unwelcome foreigner.

### Decision Triggers

- Speech or privacy concerns.
- Medical-policy concerns.
- Surveillance or censorship worries.
- Cultural alienation.

### Countries They May Already Know

Portugal, Mexico, Switzerland, Costa Rica.

### Countries They May Overlook

Uruguay, Chile, Georgia, Czechia, Slovenia.

### Product Needs

- Civil liberties, digital freedom, and legal-system detail.
- Foreigner backlash category.
- Psychological fit.
- Culture/language notes.

## 8. Retiree or Aging Planner

### Context

This user may be nearing retirement, supporting older parents, managing chronic health issues, or seeking a more affordable and stable aging environment.

### Motivations

- Reduce retirement costs.
- Access private healthcare and home care.
- Live somewhere safer and calmer.
- Understand whether a country works at age 75, not just age 35.

### Fears

- Poor specialist care.
- Limited mobility access.
- Being far from family.
- Underestimating private insurance costs.

### Decision Triggers

- Retirement planning.
- Healthcare costs.
- Safety or loneliness concerns.
- Desire for domestic help or elder support.

### Countries They May Already Know

Mexico, Portugal, Spain, Costa Rica, Panama.

### Countries They May Overlook

Uruguay, Chile, Malaysia, Slovenia, Colombia.

### Product Needs

- Family and Aging profile.
- Healthcare, aging suitability, safety, and connectivity.
- City-level healthcare caveats.
- Elder-care source gaps.

## 9. Frontier Upside Seeker

### Context

This user is willing to accept volatility for opportunity. They may be a founder, investor, operator, real estate buyer, or builder looking for underpriced countries.

### Motivations

- Identify asymmetric upside.
- Compare risk/reward across overlooked markets.
- Find reform momentum, resource security, and low costs.
- Avoid influencer hype and naive frontier optimism.

### Fears

- Currency collapse.
- Weak courts.
- Political instability.
- Being too early, too late, or wrong about reform.

### Decision Triggers

- Reform news.
- Asset-price dislocation.
- New residency or investment pathway.
- Desire to operate in an underbuilt market.

### Countries They May Already Know

Argentina, Paraguay, Colombia, Georgia, Panama.

### Countries They May Overlook

Uruguay as a stable base, Chile for infrastructure/resource exposure, lesser-known Eastern European countries.

### Product Needs

- Frontier Upside profile.
- Risk tolerance fit.
- Currency/banking and tail risk.
- Opportunity versus daily friction.
- Data quality / reality gap warnings.

## 10. Research Editor

### Context

This user maintains the country dataset. They may be the site owner, analyst, contributor, or reviewer.

### Motivations

- Keep country files consistent.
- Find stale or weak categories quickly.
- Prevent demo data from being mistaken for researched data.
- Preserve source notes, confidence, caveats, and review status.

### Fears

- Publishing misleading scores.
- Losing track of source freshness.
- Letting rankings imply false precision.
- Allowing monetization to corrupt methodology.

### Decision Triggers

- Adding a new country.
- Updating a country after law or policy changes.
- Preparing a paid brief.
- Reviewing an LLM-generated draft.

### Countries They May Already Know

All countries in the dataset.

### Countries They May Overlook

Countries with weak public narratives but strong category-specific fit.

### Product Needs

- Research queue.
- Review dashboard.
- Data quality scoring.
- Validation.
- Source freshness.
- Editorial checklist.

## Persona Prioritization

For MVP and early monetization, prioritize:

1. Plan B American
2. Remote Worker / Entrepreneur
3. Wealth Preservation Planner
4. Family Stability Seeker
5. Research Editor

Climate/resilience, retirees, freedom migrants, and frontier seekers should remain supported through weighting profiles and category visibility, but they can become more specialized paid report lines later.

## Design Implications

- The app should let users start from a concern, not only from a country.
- The ranking should always be profile-specific.
- Country pages should explain why a score exists.
- Unknown or unfamiliar countries should be introduced in plain language.
- Confidence and source quality should be visible without making the interface feel academic.
- The product should feel serious and calm, not alarmist.

## Monetization Implications

- Users are more likely to pay when they face a real decision, not when they are casually browsing.
- Personalized shortlist reports are likely the highest-value early product.
- Subscriptions make sense once there are enough researched countries and update events.
- Professional tiers require stronger citations, export tools, and methodology consistency.
- Affiliate revenue should be avoided or heavily disclosed because it can undermine trust.
